MolScoreBenchmark preset descriptions
====================================

Source: summaries inferred from installed MolScore preset configs
(`MolScoreBenchmark.presets`) and their scoring components.

5HT2A_Docking
- 2 docking/selectivity tasks for 5-HT2A objectives using rDock plus descriptor constraints.

5HT2A_PhysChem
- 5 tasks combining 5-HT2A activity prediction with physicochemical property and synthesizability controls.

5HT2A_Selectivity
- 6 selectivity tasks driven by PIDGIN predictors (e.g., 5-HT2A vs off-target profiles) plus descriptor constraints.

GuacaMol
- 20 standard GuacaMol goal-directed tasks (rediscovery, similarity, isomer, MPO, SMARTS/scaffold objectives).

GuacaMol_Scaffold
- 4 scaffold-focused GuacaMol tasks emphasizing scaffold matching, similarity, and property constraints.

LibINVENT_Exp1
- 2 LibINVENT-style tasks with decorated reaction/substructure constraints and DRD2-focused QSAR components.

LinkINVENT_Exp3
- 11 linker-design tasks with linker descriptor objectives (length/shape/property windows) and base molecular constraints.

MolExp
- 5 molecular extrapolation tasks (A2A/AP/BACE1/EGFR style setups) using similarity plus descriptor constraints.

MolExpL
- 5 MolExp-style tasks using mostly Levenshtein-based sequence similarity with descriptor constraints.

MolExpL_baseline
- 12 baseline MolExpL tasks (multiple seeds/splits per target) using Levenshtein similarity and descriptors.

MolExp_baseline
- 12 baseline MolExp tasks (multiple seeds/splits per target) using molecular similarity and descriptors.

MolOpt
- 23 molecule-optimization tasks, extending GuacaMol-like objectives with additional optimization/QSAR scenarios.

MolOpt-CF
- 23 MolOpt tasks with an added ChemistryFilter component (stricter chemistry validity/quality filtering).

MolOpt-DF
- 23 MolOpt tasks configured for diversity-filtered optimization (DF variant of MolOpt settings).
